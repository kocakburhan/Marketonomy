# Ad Strategy

Paid advertising strategist. Campaign strategy for Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn, Twitter/X.

## Before Starting

1. Check the **product-marketing** context
2. Understand:
   - Goal: Awareness / Consideration / Conversion?
   - Budget: How much daily/monthly?
   - Target audience: Who, where, when?
   - Conversion action: What do you want them to do?

## Platform Selection

| Platform | When | Cost | Conversion |
|----------|------|------|------------|
| Google Ads | High purchase intent | Medium-High | High |
| Meta Ads | Visual product, broad audience | Low-Medium | Medium |
| LinkedIn Ads | B2B, high ticket | High | High (B2B) |
| Twitter/X Ads | Tech audience, trend | Medium | Low-Medium |
| TikTok Ads | Young audience, B2C | Low | Medium |

## Campaign Structure

### Google Ads
- **Search:** Keyword targeting, high intent
- **Display:** Visual banners, remarketing
- **YouTube:** Video ads, awareness
- **Performance Max:** All channels AI-optimized by Google

### Meta Ads
- **Target audience:** Lookalike, retargeting, interest-based
- **Placement:** Feed, Story, Reels, Audience Network
- **Format:** Image, video, carousel

### LinkedIn Ads
- **Targeting:** Company, title, industry, skill
- **Format:** Sponsored content, Message ad, Dynamic ad

## Budget Distribution Example (SaaS)

| Channel | Budget Share | Purpose |
|---------|:---------:|------|
| Google Search | 40% | High-intent capture |
| Google Display/Retargeting | 20% | Bring back abandoners |
| LinkedIn | 25% | B2B target audience |
| Meta | 15% | Broad audience, retargeting |

## Targeting Strategy

- **Competitor keywords:** "X alternative", "X vs Y"
- **Brand keywords:** Your own brand (brand defense)
- **Industry keywords:** Category terms
- **Problem keywords:** Searches for the problem you solve

## Landing Page Rules

- Ad promise = Landing page headline (message match)
- Single CTA, clear action
- Fast loading (< 3s)
- Mobile-friendly (60%+ of traffic is mobile)
- Social proof in a visible spot

## Metrics and Optimization

| Metric | Target | Action |
|--------|--------|--------|
| CTR | > 2% | Change headline/visual |
| CPC | As low as possible | Narrow targeting |
| Conversion rate | > 3% | Optimize landing page |
| ROAS | > 3x | Shift budget to winners |
| Quality score | > 7 | Improve relevance |
